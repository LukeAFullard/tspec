Reading these results

  • With the new seasonal window, tspec beats STL on irregular sampling for the seasonal, remainder and trend components, and no longer fails on clustered sampling.
  • When the seasonal shape is fixed for the whole record, one pooled harmonic fit is 1.3 to 2.4 times more precise for the seasonal component. tspec wins whenever the shape changes over time. Set seasonal_window to the record length if you believe the shape is stable.
  • WRTDS-style regression tracks smooth trends well but cannot follow an 8-year cycle with its 7-year time window, and its single harmonic misses peaked seasonal shapes. Real WRTDS also uses river discharge, which this comparison leaves out.
  • On the two real records the methods are statistically indistinguishable in held-out prediction. The pooled harmonic model is marginally best, which suggests these rivers' seasonal shapes are fairly stable.
  • Seasonal bands under-cover strongly evolving shapes (86 to 89%), and red noise with two time scales was not part of this test (it under-covered in validation 1).
